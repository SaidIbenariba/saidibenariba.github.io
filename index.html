How I work

I start from the constraint, not the model. At Orange Business the network was air-gapped and the invoices came in 1,000+ carrier formats. Those two facts shaped the system more than any model benchmark did.

I don’t trust a number I haven’t broken down. Several seeds, one ablation per claim, errors read by hand and counted by slice. If a gain disappears on the second run, it was never a gain. I wrote about this.

The data pipeline matters more than the architecture. On Arabic hate speech, normalization and tokenization for dialect mattered more than which BERT I fine-tuned. For low-resource languages, the preprocessing is the moat.

I ship. I co-founded illico.ma and own its stack end to end, from the Next.js app to CI/CD on AWS. I also build local document AI (invoices, orders, contracts) that runs with no cloud API.

I think in three languages. Darija, French and English, often in the same sentence. That’s why code-switched speech is the research problem I want to work on.

Now

Founder and full-stack developer at illico.ma. Before that, I spent six months taking a vision-language document validation system to production at Orange Business Morocco.

Research interests (exploring). Speech and NLP for Moroccan Darija and code-switched French–Arabic–English, document understanding under constrained compute, and evaluation methodology.
